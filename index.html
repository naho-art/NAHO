About

Artist NAHO

Hello! My name is NAHO, and I am a painter based in Baden-Württemberg, Germany. Through my art, I strive to notice the beauty in everyday life — to capture fleeting moments and preserve their atmosphere on canvas. I draw inspiration from the natural world around me: animals, plants, and landscapes all become a source of emotion and reflection in my work. Painting is my way of expressing love and appreciation for the world we live in. I mainly work with oil and acrylic on canvas, although I enjoy experimenting with new materials and techniques from time to time. Each painting carries a small part of my personal journey, and I am truly grateful that my work resonates with others. Thank you for your interest in my art. I hope that one day at least one of my paintings will find a special place in your home.

Every original exists as a single unique piece — one painting, one owner.
