About

Artist NAHO

Hello! My name is NAHO, and I am a painter based in Baden-Württemberg, Germany. Through my art, I strive to notice the beauty in everyday life — to capture fleeting moments and preserve their atmosphere on canvas. I draw inspiration from the natural world around me — animals, plants, and landscapes — but also from personal memory and the quiet moments of everyday life. All become a source of emotion and reflection in my work. Painting is my way of expressing love and appreciation for the world we live in. I work primarily in oil. My work sits within the realm of Contemporary Impressionism, with elements of Lyrical Realism and Personal Narrative. Each painting carries a small part of my personal journey, and I am truly grateful that my work resonates with others. Thank you for your interest in my art. I hope that one day at least one of my paintings will find a special place in your home.
